Hour 36 Safe Refeeding Strategy

Avoid large carb loads immediately after fasting to prevent insulin spikes and rebound fat storage:

Stage 1 (Minute 0–45): Digestive Awakening

Warm bone broth with salt, or 1–2 boiled eggs with a few cucumber slices. Starts digestive enzymes without spiking insulin.

Stage 2 (After 60–90 mins): Primary Nutrient Meal

Lean chicken breast, salmon, or tofu alongside fibrous greens (spinach, asparagus, zucchini) and extra virgin olive oil or avocado.

Avoid During Refeed Window

Bread, pasta, refined sugary treats, and dairy milk. Insulin sensitivity is high, meaning refined carbohydrates are stored as lipids more readily.
